Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about contacting Mjamaa Bank. If you do not see your question here, please reach out to us directly using any of the methods above.

How do I open a Mjamaa Bank account?

You can open an account in three ways. Visit any branch with your original ID, passport, and a recent utility bill. Open the Mjamaa mobile app and complete digital onboarding with a video selfie verification. Or apply online through our website for certain account types. The digital process takes less than 10 minutes and you will receive your account number instantly.

What documents do I need to visit a branch?

Bring your original national ID or passport for identification. If you are opening a joint account, both account holders must be present. For business accounts, bring your certificate of incorporation, ID for all directors, and the company PIN. A recent utility bill or bank statement dated within 90 days is also required as proof of address for all new customers.

How do I report a lost or stolen card?

Call our 24 hour Contact Center immediately at +254 700 000 000. Your card will be blocked instantly and a replacement will be issued. You can also block your card yourself using the mobile app within the Card Services section. There is no fee for reporting a lost or stolen card, and our support team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week to assist you.

Can I apply for a loan online or over the phone?

Yes. You can apply for personal loans, auto loans, and mortgage financing through our online banking portal or mobile app. Loan applications can also be submitted over the phone by speaking with one of our loan officers. Most personal loan decisions are made within minutes and funds can be transferred to your Mjamaa account instantly upon approval. For larger loan products, a branch visit may be required for document signing.

What should I do if I forget my mobile app PIN?

If you forget your PIN, select the Reset PIN option on the login screen of the mobile app. You will be prompted to verify your identity using your national ID or by answering your security questions. Once verified, you can set a new PIN immediately. If you are unable to reset your PIN through the app, call our Contact Center for assisted reset. For security reasons, your PIN can only be reset after successful identity verification.

Do you offer currency exchange services at branches?

Yes, all our branches offer currency exchange services for major international currencies including USD, GBP, EUR, JPY, and others. Exchange rates are updated daily and displayed at the counter and in the mobile app. You can also place foreign exchange orders through online banking for amounts above 50,000 KES. There is a small commission on currency exchanges, which is disclosed upfront before any transaction is processed.

How long does it take to receive a response to my message?

Messages submitted through our contact form, secure messaging portal, or email are typically answered within one business day. Phone and live chat inquiries receive immediate attention during operating hours. For urgent matters involving suspected fraud or unauthorized transactions, call our 24 hour Contact Center immediately for real time assistance and to ensure your account stays protected.
