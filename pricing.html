Transparent Pricing You Can Trust

At Mjamaa Bank, we believe that knowing the cost of your banking services should never be a mystery. Our pricing is designed to be fair, straightforward, and fully disclosed so you always know exactly what you are paying for. Whether you are opening your first savings account, applying for a home loan, or upgrading to a premium card, our transparent fee structure ensures there are no surprises along the way.

Our Pricing Philosophy

Mjamaa Bank charges fees only where required by regulation or where a service involves a clear cost to deliver. We publish our full tariff schedule annually and notify customers of any changes at least 30 days in advance. Account holders on higher-tier packages enjoy progressive benefits including reduced or waived fees on most transactions. All fees are inclusive of applicable taxes unless stated otherwise, and the most up-to-date prices are always available in our mobile app, online banking portal, and at any branch.

Loan Interest Rates

Our loan pricing is competitive and transparent. Rates are expressed as annual percentage rates (APR) and are determined by your credit profile, loan amount, and repayment term. The ranges below reflect what most qualified borrowers can expect to receive.

Personal Loan

12% - 18% p.a.

Unsecured personal financing for debt consolidation, medical expenses, or major purchases. Terms from 6 months to 5 years. Processing fee of up to 2% of the principal amount.

Salary Loan

10% - 15% p.a.

Fast approval for salaried individuals with direct deposit. Interest calculated on a reducing balance basis. Terms from 3 months to 3 years.

Home Loan

10% - 14% p.a.

Competitive financing for property purchase, construction, or refinancing. Terms of up to 25 years with flexible repayment options. Legal and valuation fees apply.

Car Loan

11% - 16% p.a.

Finance for new or used vehicle purchases from licensed dealers. Terms from 12 months to 7 years. Requires comprehensive insurance coverage.

Business Loan

13% - 19% p.a.

Working capital and expansion financing for registered businesses. Terms from 6 months to 5 years. Requires business financial statements and security documentation.

Education Loan

11% - 17% p.a.

Funding for tuition fees, books, and accommodation at recognized institutions. Terms aligned with the academic calendar with a six-month grace period after graduation.

All loan rates are subject to change based on market conditions and individual eligibility. Final rates are communicated during the application process after a credit assessment. Late payment fees of up to 5% of the outstanding amount may apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about our pricing, fees, and account features.

Can I switch between account packages at any time?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your account package at any time through our mobile app, online banking portal, or by visiting any branch. If you downgrade to a lower-tier package, the change takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Any fees already charged for the current month are non-refundable. If you are upgrading, your new benefits take effect immediately and the new monthly fee will be charged from your next statement.

How do I know which fees apply to my account?

Your monthly account statement shows a detailed breakdown of all fees charged during the statement period. You can also view your personalized fee summary in the Fees section of our mobile app or online banking. For a complete list of all fees, you can download the full Mjamaa Bank Tariff Schedule PDF, which is updated annually and available on our website, in all our branches, and within your account dashboard.

Are there any hidden fees not listed here?

Absolutely not. Mjamaa Bank operates on a policy of full fee transparency. Every fee or charge that applies to your account will appear on your monthly statement with a clear description. We provide regulatory notices at least 30 days before introducing or changing any fee, and you will receive an email notification as well. If you see a charge on your statement that you do not recognize, contact our customer service team immediately and we will investigate it for you.

What happens if my balance falls below the minimum?

If your account balance falls below the minimum required for your package, you will receive a notification via SMS and in the mobile app 7 days before the fee is applied. You then have a grace period of 7 days to bring your balance above the minimum. If your balance remains below the threshold after the grace period, the monthly management fee for your package will be charged, and your account may be reviewed for a package downgrade to better suit your balance level. We are always happy to discuss switching you to a more cost-effective package.

Do you offer discounts for students or seniors?

Yes. Students enrolled at recognized Kenyan universities can apply for our Student Banking package, which waives the monthly management fee and includes a free debit card with reduced ATM limits. Seniors aged 65 and above receive a 50% discount on most service fees, including ATM withdrawals, transfers, and account maintenance. To qualify, you simply need to provide valid identification and proof of age or enrolment. These discounts are available across all our digital and branch channels once your status has been verified and documented in your account profile.

How are loan interest rates determined?

Loan interest rates are determined by a combination of factors including your credit score, income level, employment history, loan amount, repayment term, and the type of loan you are applying for. We use a risk-based pricing model, which means that borrowers with stronger credit profiles receive lower rates. All applicants receive a personalized rate quote during the application process after a soft credit check, which does not affect your credit score. The final rate is locked in once your loan is approved and the agreement is signed.

Can I avoid loan processing fees?

Premium account holders enjoy waived processing fees on all loan products, including personal loans, car loans, and business financing. For other customers, processing fees range from 1% to 2% of the principal amount depending on the loan type. These fees are disclosed upfront in your loan application and are included in your total cost of borrowing. You will never be charged a processing fee that was not clearly communicated to you before you signed your loan agreement.

What is your policy on fee refunds?

If you are charged a fee in error, or if a service you were charged for was not delivered as promised, you are entitled to a full refund. To request a refund, contact our customer service team within 30 days of the charge appearing on your statement. If your refund request is approved, the amount will be credited back to your account within 5 business days. For recurring fees such as monthly management charges, refunds are processed as account credits that reduce your next statement balance.

Our Commitment to Transparency

Mjamaa Bank is committed to making our pricing clear and accessible to every customer. We publish our complete tariff schedule annually, and any changes to fees are communicated to customers at least 30 days in advance through SMS, email, and notices posted in our mobile app and online banking portal.

We also maintain a dedicated fee calculator tool, available for free in our mobile app and on our website, that allows you to estimate the cost of any transaction or service before you initiate it. If you ever notice a charge on your statement that does not match your understanding of our fees, our customer service team is ready to investigate and resolve the matter promptly.

  • All fees are inclusive of applicable taxes unless stated otherwise. Exchange rates and spreads are updated daily and are available in real time through our digital channels.
  • Customers are notified of any fee changes through at least two channels: SMS to the registered mobile number and a push notification in the Mjamaa mobile app.
  • The complete Mjamaa Bank Tariff Schedule (Form CBK 22) is available for download as a PDF from our website, at every branch location, and within the Resources section of your online account.
  • Regulatory fees imposed by the Central Bank of Kenya or the Kenya Deposit Insurance Fund are passed through to customers without additional markup. We do not add administrative surcharges to these mandated charges.
  • Premium account holders receive personalized fee summaries and proactive alerts when approaching any threshold that would trigger a fee on their account.
