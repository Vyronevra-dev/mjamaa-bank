About Mjamaa Bank

Mjamaa Bank is a licensed commercial bank in Kenya, proudly regulated by the Central Bank of Kenya. We exist to grow together with our customers, communities, and partners — turning everyday financial needs into long-term prosperity.

Our Foundation

Mission

To provide secure, accessible, and innovative financial services that empower individuals, businesses, and communities to achieve their goals and build lasting wealth — anchored in trust, transparency, and responsible banking.

Vision

To be Kenya's most trusted and forward-thinking financial partner — a bank that everyone can bank on, everywhere, for every stage of life.

Values

We operate on four pillars: Customer First, putting clients at the center of everything we do; Integrity, holding ourselves to the highest ethical standards; Innovation, constantly reimagining how banking can serve better; and Community, investing in the places where we live and work.

Our Story

Founded in 2018 by a group of seasoned Kenyan banking professionals, Mjamaa Bank began with a simple but powerful idea: banking should work for everyone, not just a few. The name "Mjamaa" — which means "family" in Swahili — reflects our belief that a bank's true strength comes from the relationships it nurtures and the communities it serves.

From our first branch in Nairobi's central business district, we set out to build a bank that combined the reliability and security of traditional banking with the convenience and innovation of modern fintech. Over the years, we have expanded across the country, introduced award-winning digital services, and launched initiatives that go far beyond finance — from education scholarships to grassroots sports development.

Today, Mjamaa Bank serves over 500,000 customers through a network of branches, agents, and digital channels. We are regulated by the Central Bank of Kenya (Banking License No. BL/001/2024), a member of the Kenya Deposit Insurance Fund, and committed to the highest standards of corporate governance.

We do not see banking as a transaction. We see it as a relationship — one that grows over time, adapts to changing needs, and helps every family and business build a stronger future. This is who we are, and this is why we grow together.

Regulatory & Compliance

Mjamaa Bank Ltd. is a duly licensed commercial bank regulated by the Central Bank of Kenya (CBK). Our banking license (No. BL/001/2024) authorizes us to accept deposits and make loans across the Republic of Kenya. We are also a member of the Kenya Deposit Insurance Fund (KIDF), which protects eligible deposits up to KES 500,000 per depositor.

We maintain robust risk management frameworks, conduct regular internal and external audits, and comply with all applicable laws including the Banking Act (Cap 11), the Central Bank of Kenya Act, the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism (Financial Institutions) Act, and the Data Protection Act, 2019.

Customer deposits are held separate from operational funds, our capital adequacy ratio is reviewed quarterly against CBK requirements, and all board decisions are documented following strict corporate governance guidelines. We are committed to transparency, and full details of our regulatory standing, financial statements, and risk disclosures are available in our Investor Relations section.
