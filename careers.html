Build Your Future With Us

At Mjamaa Bank, we believe that the best way to grow together is to invest in the people who make it happen. From fresh graduates to seasoned professionals, we offer meaningful careers in a bank that puts people at the center of everything — our customers, our communities, and our team.

Why Join Mjamaa Bank

Our strength comes from our people. We are guided by four commitments that shape how we work, grow, and support one another — every single day.

Customer First

We make decisions with our customers' best interests in mind. Every product, every process, and every interaction is designed to earn and keep the trust that makes banking personal.

Growth Mindset

We invest in your development through mentorship programs, tuition assistance, professional certifications, and clear pathways to advance across functions and geographies.

Inclusive Culture

From employee resource groups to flexible work arrangements, we create space for every voice to be heard, valued, and empowered to contribute its best.

What We Offer

We believe in supporting our team members holistically — protecting their health, securing their futures, and rewarding their contributions with benefits that go beyond the basics.

Comprehensive Health Cover

Medical, dental, and optical insurance for you and your registered dependents, with access to a nationwide network of providers and cashless outpatient care.

Retirement Savings

A generous pension contribution matched up to regulatory limits, supplemented by a provident fund that helps you build long-term financial security.

Learning & Development

Up to 15 paid training days per year, full reimbursement for approved professional qualifications, and access to our leadership development programmes.

Work Life Flexibility

Hybrid and remote options where roles allow, flexible working hours, generous annual leave, and 18 paid public holidays plus an extra day for your birthday.

Life at Mjamaa

Our workplace is built on collaboration, growth, and purpose. Here, you will find an environment that supports your wellbeing, celebrates your contributions, and gives you the tools to thrive both professionally and personally.

Flexible Work

Choose how and where you work. With hybrid options, flexible hours, and remote-friendly roles, we trust you to deliver results on a schedule that works for your life and responsibilities.

Mentorship Programs

Accelerate your growth with structured mentorship from senior leaders, peer coaching circles, and reverse mentoring that connects experienced staff with fresh perspectives from early-career talent.

Innovation Time

Dedicate 10% of your work hours to passion projects and experiment Fridays, where cross-functional teams build prototypes, pilot new ideas, and solve real customer challenges together.
